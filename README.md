# Wolverine Rush

For years, the shadows of the **Weapon X** program haunted Logan, a black-ops nightmare of cold steel, wiped memories, and unbreakable adamantium. When intelligence surfaced that a covert convoy was transporting the program's final encrypted server drives out of a hidden facility, Wolverine set out into the night with a single objective: intercept the cargo and burn the project's blueprints to ash before another generation of killers could be made.

He tracked the armored transport truck to the edge of the city, only to discover someone else found it first. **Deadpool** had already hijacked the transport, aiming to auction the classified Weapon X drives to the highest bidder on the black market.

With sirens wailing in the distance and the truck tearing down the city highway, a personal vendetta erupts into a relentless, high-speed street chase. Refusing to let the truck pull away, Wolverine breaks into a full-throttle sprint right down the asphalt behind the tailgate.

## Gameplay & Mechanics

Trying to hold off the pursuit from the speeding vehicle, Deadpool treats the chase like a twisted game:

* **Heavy Cargo Drops:** Whenever Logan closes the distance, Wade hauls heavy, reinforced wooden crates out of the cargo hold and drops them onto the road, forcing Wolverine to perform quick jumps (`W`) over them.
* **High-Speed Blade Throws:** When crates aren't enough to break Logan's stride, Deadpool draws his twin katanas and sends them spinning through the air at head height, giving Wolverine a split second to drop into a low crouch (`S`) beneath the flying steel.

Fueled by pure determination and his mutant healing factor, Wolverine pushes through the concrete gauntlet. Every impact forces his body into overdrive, giving him a brief surge of resilience before the damage adds up. **Three direct hits** will temporarily overload his healing factor and knock him out of the chase, letting Deadpool disappear into the city skyline with the Weapon X files still in his hands.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **W** | Jump |
